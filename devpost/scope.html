Skill Pack Guide (working title)

A bilingual (English–Vietnamese), interactive website that walks people step by step through the Build With AI: Basics skill pack: where to go, which command to type, what they'll see. Only after that does it explain the method behind it and the tips.

The kernel: Learn by trying it on the page, not just reading. The user steps through a simulated walkthrough and sees in advance what they'll type and how the agent will respond, before doing it for real on their own machine.
"phần key và quan trọng nhất vẫn là interactive hướng dẫn người dùng cách dùng Skill ra sao, vào đâu, gõ dòng lệnh nào, rồi sau đó mới là giải thích lý do, cho best practice, Tips" The key and most important part is an interactive guide to how to use the Skills: where to go, which command to type. Who, and why

Who it's for

People who want to build products with AI and have plenty of ideas but don't know where to start or what flow of thinking to follow. Includes beginners and coders who lack a systematic method for building an MVP.

What they do today

Watch long English-language videos or read the hackathon page and figure it out themselves.

Why you're building it

"người dùng đọc lại là biết cách dùng Skill như thế nào…"
Readers come away knowing how to use the Skills, how to use them effectively, the method behind them, and concrete examples.

What "working" looks like

Open the site in a browser → switch EN/VI → go through the whole interactive walkthrough from creating a folder to /6-ship, each step with a copyable command and a simulated result → click a step in the flow diagram to open its details → in the "why" section, drag decision cards between "AI" and "You" and watch an app preview change.

The "oh, that's cool" moment: the viewer watches the whole 6-step journey "run" in front of them as if they were using it for real.

Proof-of-concept boundary

  • 1Interactive step-by-step walkthrough: where to go, which command to type (copyable), simulated response.
  • 2Clickable 6-step flow diagram. Each step shows what it does, which file it creates in devpost/, a tip, and a concrete example.
  • 3Why: flipped interaction, cognitive debt, writing things down as memory, building in small steps, plus the "Who makes the decisions?" drag-and-drop.
  • 4Best practices & tips: speech-to-text, /clear between steps, write your own submission, never expose API keys…
  • ✓Bilingual EN/VI with a toggle.
  • ✓Small sound effects (click / drop / complete) with a mute toggle.
  • Charts (typing 40 vs. speaking 130 words per minute, etc.).
  • Background music.
  • Mini-game: drag the 6 steps into the right order; comprehension checks.
  • Running real commands in the browser: simulation only. Users run the real thing on their own machine, which is safe and simple.
  • Copying video transcripts verbatim: to respect copyright, we rewrite in our own words and link to the videos.
  • Accounts/login, backend, saved progress: not needed to prove the kernel.
